# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and managers of small businesses, sole traders, startups and individuals, mainly around Northampton, Kettering, Milton Keynes and Rugby (Northamptonshire, Warwickshire, Buckinghamshire), plus remote clients UK-wide. They have no internal IT department and do not want one. They arrive with a problem or project (a new starter's laptop, a messy Microsoft 365 tenant, spreadsheets nobody can read, a new office or website) and want to know quickly whether Cevano can handle it and how to ask.

## Product Purpose

The website exists to win enquiries, by contact form (`functions/api/contact.js`) or by email to enquiries@cevano.co.uk.

Service priority for the business right now:

1. Microsoft 365 management (lead service to win).
2. Web / landing page design.
3. IT support.

Data analytics / Power BI remains a core service. Networking & AV, IT assets & documentation, and domains / DNS are secondary services.

Note: web design is currently listed only as a minor item under "Domains & Online Services". The stated priority means it may deserve more prominence. That is a content decision to confirm with the owner before any page restructure.

## Positioning

One provider for everyday IT support, Microsoft 365 administration, and data analytics with Power BI, which small businesses would otherwise source from three suppliers. Plain-language explanations and practical solutions sized for small businesses, not enterprise IT. Local on-site presence around Northamptonshire, with remote support where it makes sense.

## Operating Context

- Visitors usually come from local search, referrals, or a shared link, often on mobile.
- The enquiry path is: describe the problem, Cevano asks questions and explains options in plain terms, then fixes it or provides ongoing support.
- The contact form asks for name, company, phone, email, service area, message and consent. The "What to include" guidance asks for the problem, the number of people or devices, and urgency.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/JS site, English and Polish (`/pl/` mirrors every page). Any new or changed content needs both languages.
- HTML is generated by Python scripts (`build*.py`). Edit the generators, not only the output HTML.
- Hosted on Cloudflare Pages, deployed from GitHub `main`. The contact form uses a Cloudflare Pages Function and Resend. Email runs on Microsoft 365.
- Light and dark themes, with a user toggle and `prefers-color-scheme`.
- Google Analytics loads only after cookie consent. No advertising use.
- No CMS, no database. `_headers` sets security headers, so new third-party resources need CSP review.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Cevano IT Solutions. Existing logo assets are in `assets/img/` (`logo-trimmed`, `logo-white`, favicons, `og-image.jpg`).
- The business is run by one person. Copy keeps the company voice ("we"). Never state or imply a team size, staff, or departments.
- The site stays impersonal (owner's decision, 2026-11-05): no founder name, photo, or "who you'll deal with" block. Trust comes from specifics (real M365 problems, process, PL-300, reply expectations) instead.
- Voice: approachable, practical, plain English, no jargon overload, never salesy hype.
- Legal facts: Company No. 17428300. Registered address: Lytchett House, 13 Freeland Park, Wareham Road, Poole, Dorset, BH16 6FA. The registered address is a virtual office. Do not present it as a place clients visit.
- Social links: Facebook and LinkedIn (icons in `assets/img/`).

## Evidence on Hand

- Certification: Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst Associate (PL-300). This is the only certification held. Do not claim MS-900, MS-102, MD-102 or any other Microsoft 365 certification.
- No client testimonials, case studies, client logos, review scores, or statistics. Future work must not invent any of these.
- Real content: service lists and example requests on `services.html`, the three-step process on `index.html`, and the service area list on `about.html` and `contact.html`.

## Product Principles

1. Every page leads toward one clear enquiry, by form or email.
2. Plain language first: explain what the client gets, not the technology.
3. Sized for small businesses: no enterprise claims or language.
4. Truthful scale: one trusted point of contact, never a pretend team.
5. Claim only what is held: PL-300 is the only credential.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Bilingual audience (English and Polish): both languages must reach parity. The site already has a skip link and supports light and dark themes. No formal standard was stated; WCAG 2.2 AA is the working target.
